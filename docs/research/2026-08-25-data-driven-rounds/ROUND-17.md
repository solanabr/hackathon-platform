# Round 17 — The competitive deep-map

5 agents. Colosseum, DoraHacks, Devfolio, Devpost, Solana Mobile × Radiant, Align Nexus.

## Colosseum (agent 1)
- Product surface: the funnel (01 Compete → 02 Win → 03 Get funded, $250K pre-seed), the login-gated Arena, 6-track editions with sponsor-presented awards, the accelerator cohorts (C1-C4), the Eternal Challenge.
- **Judging (steal for the 09/12 banca)**: all submissions → shortlist ~150 → founder panel cuts to ~40 winners → 10-15 funded. Criteria: full-time intent + viable business model + work done DURING the hackathon + founder-market fit + traction. **The pitch video is #1** — "a brief startup pitch, not a product demo," max 3 min.
- Retention: the Eternal Challenge + the cross-edition trajectory ("Learners become Contributors become Founders").
- Moat: pipeline-to-capital + reputation. Gaps we own: no public gallery, no in-platform team formation, no permanent content archive, no community comms layer.

## DoraHacks + Devfolio (agent 2)
- DoraHacks moat = funding rails (quadratic funding, grants) + AI judging; Devfolio moat = organizer ops (the review/RSVP/check-in flow, the microsite, the builder graph in India).
- **Steal**: per-track judge assignment (DoraHacks), accept/reject/waitlist + bulk review states + RSVP→check-in gating + microsite branding (Devfolio), winner announcements + public project archive (both).
- **Overkill**: on-chain voting, QF/grant rails, SBTs, staking, AI judging, sponsor ecosystems.
- Ours better: the pt-BR LP language, the content archive as the living agenda, the in-person reality.

## Devpost + radiant + align (agent 3)
- **Steal**: the winner banner at the top of the gallery; the up-to-6-criteria 1-5-star rubric with auto-averaged scores + ranked winner-picking; sponsor-tiered prize blocks; an explicit Voting phase; the submission-as-link-survey (builder-identity capture).
- **Converged "ultimate" patterns (8)**: public gallery + winner highlighting; tracks + sponsor tiers; explicit timeline incl. community voting; criteria-based judged averaging; builder identity (persisting portfolios); the community loop (likes/comments/votes); distinct design energy; after-event retention (announcements, archive).
- M1-M6 coverage gaps: no winner-banner gallery highlighting (only M6 featured), no voting phase, no per-criterion judging UI.

## Feature matrix (agent 4)
- Ours-planned reaches parity on event-scoped features (gallery, judging, archive, sponsors) and deliberately skips the marketplace layer.
- **GAPS still missed by M1-M6**: gallery search/filter/categories (every benchmark has it); profile trajectory (no placements, no non-leader projects, no hall-of-fame — the round-8 H1/H2 data-only fixes); WhatsApp-native team invites; the in-app retention surface; mobile/share (gallery links share as image-less cards).
- Overkill: funding rails, cofounder matching, the eternal challenge, bounties, i18n/multi-currency.
- Differentiators: the LP-branded content archive as the event's living agenda; in-person Pitch Day support; the WhatsApp-native flow; the pt-BR community.

## The ultimate definition (agent 5)
- **Thesis**: neither Colosseum's funding pipeline, DoraHacks' grants machine, nor Devpost's global bazaar — the community's own stage and memory: the thin reliable layer between Luma and WhatsApp, in the LP's cream-and-emerald skin, that makes a pt-BR in-person mobile-first hackathon run flawlessly and feel alive.
- **5 pillars**: (1) the event runs flawlessly (the O1 reveal hinge, the self-attested Luma); (2) participants feel seen (the finalist panel — no page reads is_finalist today); (3) builders discoverable (the gallery + profiles, but no cross-edition trajectory); (4) the content is the living agenda; (5) the community is the moat (WhatsApp-native, Luma-honest).
- **North-star**: finalists learn they made the cut in-app within an hour of the announcement (the O1 fix); 3-year: the cross-edition return rate.

## Where round 18 focuses
The community layer design: hall-of-fame, trajectories, the engagement loop, the retention surface.
